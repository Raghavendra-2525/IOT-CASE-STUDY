# Future of IoT in Smart Cities – Case Study

This project presents a detailed case study on the future of the Internet of Things (IoT) in smart cities. The report explains how IoT technologies improve transportation, healthcare, energy management, security, waste management, and environmental monitoring in modern urban environments.

## Topics Covered
- Introduction to IoT
- Smart City Concepts
- IoT Applications in Smart Cities
- Smart Transportation Systems
- Smart Healthcare
- Smart Energy Management
- IoT Security Challenges
- AI and IoT Integration
- Future Scope of Smart Cities

## Features
- Detailed 10-page case study
- Real-world examples of smart cities
- Benefits and challenges of IoT
- Easy-to-understand explanation
- PDF format report

## Technologies Discussed
- Internet of Things (IoT)
- Artificial Intelligence (AI)
- 5G Networks
- Cloud Computing
- Smart Sensors

## Applications
- Smart Traffic Management
- Environmental Monitoring
- Public Safety Systems
- Waste Management
- Smart Healthcare

## Conclusion
IoT is transforming traditional cities into smart and sustainable environments by improving efficiency, automation, and resource management. The future of smart cities depends on the successful integration of IoT technologies.
